## 0. The one-paragraph takeaway

The PH retail investing market is in a **land-grab moment**: PSE accounts jumped
**+50% in a single year to 2.86M in 2024** ✔, and the fastest-growing cohort is
**18–29-year-olds** ✔ — digital-native, mobile-first, and learning to invest from
**TikTok/"FinTok"** ✔. But **investment awareness sits at just 53%** ✔ and new
investors are buying on tips without knowing **what a stock is worth** or **when
news affects them**. That gap — *lots of new, under-informed, socially-taught
investors* — is precisely FinSight's wedge (valuation + "never get blindsided"),
and social video is exactly where they are. The campaigns should ride the growth,
speak Taglish to the 18–29 cohort, and win on *education + awareness*, not tips.

report credits its own PSE EASy/EQUIP digital rails ⚠ — attribution differs by
source, so treat "which app drove it" as unsettled).

## 2. Who the investor is → validates the personas

**Age mix (2024, total accounts)** ✔ — PSE 2024:
- **30–44: 48.8%** — the biggest block (our *blindsided holder* / *spreadsheet valuer*).
- **18–29: 26.5%** — up sharply from 19.5% the year before; **28.4% of *online* accounts** (our *COL beginner*, the paid/organic sweet spot).
- 45–59 and 60+ make up the rest; retirees (60+) grew notably in 2023 ✔.

**Implication for campaigns:**
- The **18–29 surge** is the single most important number for channel choice — it's why **TikTok leads** the plan (§3). These are new investors forming habits *now*.
- The **30–44 plurality** is reachable on **Facebook/IG** and is where the
  *valuation* and *blindsided* angles (higher stakes, real money) land hardest.
- ▶ Both cohorts skew mobile and social-taught, which is why "awareness, not
  advice" education outperforms hard product pitches.

## 4. The awareness gap — FinSight's actual wedge

| Metric | Value | Tag | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Financial literacy rate, 2025 | 74% (up from 69% in 2021) | ✔ | BSP, via Manila Standard |
| **Awareness of *investments*** | **53%** | ✔ | BSP 2025 CFIS |
| Adult account ownership | 50% | ⚠ | BSP 2025 CFIS (secondary) |
| Gen Z (1/3) get financial tips from **social media**, not advisors | qualitative | ⚠ | finfluencer coverage (Tripzilla/others) |
| Finfluencer engagement rates | ~3–5% | ⚠ | micro-influencer trackers |

**Read:** basic literacy is climbing, but **only ~half the adult population is even
aware of investing**, and those who do invest increasingly learn from **social
video, not professionals**. That produces exactly the customer FinSight is built
for: *enthusiastic but under-equipped* — they buy on tips, don't value what they
own, and get blindsided by news. The "FinTok" habit is the acquisition channel
*and* the reason the not-advice/education framing works: we meet them where they
already learn, but give them **the source and the math** instead of a hot tip.

## 5. Competitive landscape & the whitespace

| Player | What it is | Angle | Tag |
|---|---|---|---|
| **COL Financial** | Incumbent online broker; trading + research | Execution + research portal; you trade here | ✔ |
| **GStocksPH** (AB Capital via **GCash**) | In-app trading inside GCash; drove the 2023 account surge | Frictionless mass-market *buying* | ✔ |
| **GoTyme / PH Stocks** (via DragonFi) | Bank-app stock trading | Neobank distribution | ✔ |
| **Investagrams / Investa** | Social-financial platform: learn, practice, charts, community | **1M+ users**; charting + social + education | ✔ |
| **Filipino finfluencers ("FinTok")** | Creators teaching budgeting/investing | Free education + tips (some skirt "advice") | ⚠ |

**The whitespace FinSight owns** ▶:
- The incumbents are **brokers (execution)** or **charting/social (Investagrams)**.
  None center on the two things FinSight does: **rigorous PH-calibrated valuation**
  ("what is it *worth*") and **portfolio-aware news awareness** ("what just
  happened to *what I own*").
- Finfluencers give tips but carry **advice risk**; FinSight's **"awareness, not
  advice"** contract is a *differentiator*, not a limitation — it's the credible,
  compliant alternative to "buy this stock" content.
- **Positioning line ▶:** *the broker lets you buy; the finfluencer gives you a
  tip; FinSight tells you what it's worth and warns you when the news hits it.*
- **Complement, not compete:** most of these users **already have** a COL/GStocks/
  GoTyme account — FinSight is the *layer on top*, which lowers the acquisition
  ask (no "switch brokers," just "know more").

## 6. What the research tells the campaigns

| Finding | Campaign implication (see `social-campaigns.md`) |
|---|---|
| +50% account growth, 86% online | Ride the wave; a live mobile-first product acquiring via social is well-timed. |
| 18–29 is the surging cohort; TikTok = 40h+/mo | **TikTok-led organic**, Taglish, education-first (the "Halaga check" + vox-pop pillars). |
| 30–44 is the plurality, high stakes | **FB/IG** for valuation + "blindsided" angles; OFW segment on FB. |
| Only 53% investment awareness; social-taught | **Education, not tips.** The interview/vox-pop pillar (§6.5) surfaces the pain in real voices. |
| Finfluencers carry advice risk | **"Awareness, not advice"** is the brand moat — lean into it in every post. |
| Users already have a broker | CTA = "know what you own," not "switch." Position as a layer on top. |
