## 1. Positioning recap (what every post has to honor)

FinSight is an **investment awareness platform for Philippine Stock Exchange (PSE)
investors** with two pillars:

1. **Valuation workbench** — value any PSE company with four classic models
   (DCF, DDM, Graham, Multiples), calibrated for the PH market (PHP, local
   risk-free rate, ERP). "What is this stock actually worth?"
2. **Portfolio-aware insights** — "Never be blindsided by news that touches what
   you own." We watch PSE EDGE disclosures + PH macro (BSP, PSA) and connect
   them to your holdings.

**The non-negotiable: "awareness, not advice."** Every post must follow the same
contract as the product. We **surface, connect, and explain *possible* impact and
cite the source** — we **never** say buy / sell / "this stock will go up." This is
both a legal stance and the brand's whole differentiator (see §7).

**One-line brand promise:**
> *"Know what your PSE stocks are worth — and never get blindsided by news that hits them."*

## 2. Who we're talking to (personas)

> **Market snapshot** (see `market-research.md` for sources): PSE accounts hit
> **2.86M in 2024, +50% YoY**, **86% online**; the **18–29 cohort surged to 26.5%**
> and learns to invest on **TikTok** (40h+/mo), yet **only 53% of adults are even
> aware of investing**. New, mobile-first, social-taught, under-equipped — exactly
> FinSight's customer.

| Persona | Who | Core pain | Hook that lands |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The COL beginner** | 22–32, opened a COL Financial / GoTyme / GStocks account, owns 3–8 stocks, learns from FB groups & YouTube | "I bought because someone in a group said so. I don't actually know what it's worth or when to pay attention." | "Stop guessing. Value it in 2 minutes." |
| **The blindsided holder** | 30–45, working professional, buy-and-hold, checks portfolio rarely | Finds out about a disclosure/news *after* the price already moved | "We watch the news on your stocks so you don't have to." |
| **The spreadsheet valuer** | 28–50, finance-literate, does DCF in Excel, follows PSE closely | Manual, error-prone spreadsheets; US-calibrated templates | "PH-calibrated DCF/Graham without the broken spreadsheet." |
| **The OFW / diaspora investor** | 25–45, abroad, invests back home, timezone-blind to PSE hours | Can't track PH news in real time; feels disconnected | "Your Manila portfolio, watched while you sleep." |

Organic content will naturally reach the **COL beginner** (TikTok/IG) and the
**spreadsheet valuer** (finance-literate, shares valuation content) fastest. The
**blindsided holder** and **OFW** are easiest to reach later with paid targeting.

## 4. FACEBOOK campaigns (organic posts)

Facebook = community + trust, and where the *blindsided holder* and *OFW* live.
Post to the FinSight Page and, where allowed, into PH investing Groups (follow each
Group's self-promo rules — lead with value, not a pitch).

### FB-1 — "Blindsided" (awareness pillar)
- **Goal:** Reach + shares; introduce the core promise.
- **Format:** 15–30s feed video (vertical-safe).
- **Hook (first 3s):** *"Your stock dropped 8% today. Alam mo ba kung bakit?"*
- **Body:** Fast cuts — a disclosure headline → the ticker in a portfolio → FinSight surfacing "this could pressure your MEG" with a source link.
- **Caption:** "News moves your PSE stocks whether you're watching or not. FinSight watches PSE EDGE + PH market news and connects it to *what you actually own*. Not advice — just awareness, with the source attached. Try it free 👇"
- **Organic KPI:** shares, saves, comments, video ThruPlay rate.
- **Boost-ready?** ✅ Strong later top-of-funnel ad.

### FB-2 — "What's it really worth?" (valuation pillar)
- **Goal:** Clicks to app + saves.
- **Format:** Carousel (4–5 cards) or a short screen-recording of a valuation run.
- **Card flow:** 1) "Everyone says JFC is a good buy." 2) "But what's it *worth*?" 3) DCF result card. 4) Graham + Multiples cross-check. 5) "Value any PSE stock in minutes. Free — link below."
- **Caption:** "Four classic models — DCF, Dividend Discount, Graham, Multiples — calibrated for the PSE in pesos. Stop copying broken US spreadsheets."
- **Organic KPI:** link clicks, saves.
- **Boost-ready?** ✅ Best later conversion post.

### FB-3 — "The Weekly Watch" (free digest post)
- **Goal:** Recurring engagement + list-building (the weekly PSE digest is a real product surface — see insights.md).
- **Format:** Weekly text+image or short video post: "3 PSE disclosures that could move stocks this week."
- **Hook:** "We read PSE EDGE so you don't have to. Here are this week's 3 — awareness only, never advice."
- **CTA:** "Get it in your inbox every Monday → link in bio."
- **Organic KPI:** weekly reach, sign-ups from the post.
- **Boost-ready?** ✅ Great lead-gen ad later.

### FB-4 — "OFW: your Manila portfolio, watched while you sleep"
- **Goal:** Reach the diaspora segment; shares within OFW communities.
- **Format:** Feed video or carousel.
- **Hook:** "8 hours ahead of Manila. Your stocks don't wait for you."
- **Caption:** "FinSight watches the PSE on your schedule and pings you when news hits what you own. Wake up already knowing. Free 👇"
- **Organic KPI:** shares, comments from OFW audiences.
- **Boost-ready?** ✅ Geo-targeted ad later (KSA, UAE, HK, SG, US).

## 6.5 — "Usapang Stocks": interview / street series (cross-platform pillar)

A vox-pop / man-on-the-street interview series — the format that's winning in PH
finance content right now, and a near-perfect fit for FinSight. The magic: the
**interviewee names the pain in their own words** ("oo, overwhelmed ako sa dami
ng balita", "hindi ko alam kung magkano dapat halaga"). That's social proof of the
*problem* — far more persuasive than us asserting it — and the problem *is* the
product's wedge. Shoots on a phone, batches easily, and is inherently shareable
(people tag friends: "ikaw 'to").

Primary home: **TikTok + IG Reels + FB Reels** (same cut, re-sized). This is a
recurring *pillar*, not a one-off — it can carry 2–3 posts/week on its own.

### Episode formats (escalate as traction grows)
1. **"Alam mo ba kung magkano ka?"** — Q: *Do you invest in the PSE? What do you own? Do you know what it's actually worth?* Most can't answer → soft reveal: "FinSight tells you in 2 minutes." (Valuation pillar.)
2. **"Overwhelmed?"** — Q: *Paano mo sinusubaybayan ang balita sa stocks mo?* Answers land on "group chats / I don't / sobrang dami" → pain in their voice → "FinSight watches it for you." (Insights pillar.)
3. **"Na-blindside ka na ba?"** — Q: *Nagulat ka na ba na bumagsak ang stock mo tapos di mo alam bakit?* Nods + stories → the blindsided-holder promise.
4. **"Guess the value"** (game-y, high-share) — show a well-known PSE stock's price, ask passersby "mahal ba 'to o mura?", then reveal what the models say. Ends on "not advice — just the math."
5. **User testimonials** (once you have happy users) — real FinSight users on camera: "it pinged me about a disclosure I'd have missed."
6. **Creator / expert sit-downs** (further along) — longer interviews with PH finance creators, cut into shorts.

### Where to film
Makati / BGC / Ortigas CBD lunch crowds; universities (UP, DLSU, Ateneo, UST);
PSE / COL investing expos, seminars, and meetups; co-working spaces and condo
lobbies. Investing-Group meetups are a warm, high-signal crowd.

### Question bank (awareness-framed — Taglish)
- "Nag-i-invest ka ba sa stock market?"
- "Ilang stocks meron ka? Alam mo ba kung magkano dapat ang halaga nila?"
- "Paano mo nalalaman kapag may balitang tumama sa stocks mo?"
- "Na-overwhelm ka na ba sa dami ng data at balita?"
- "Na-blindside ka na ba ng news na tumama sa stock mo?"
- **Avoid:** "Anong dapat kong bilhin?" or anything that fishes for buy/sell picks — that turns a vox pop into an advice testimonial (breaks §7).

### Guardrails & consent (do not skip)
- Get **on-camera verbal consent + a simple release**; blur anyone who declines.
- Keep it about the *problem* (overwhelm, not knowing value), never edited to
  imply a person endorses buying a specific stock.
- Don't script or coach subjects into "buy X" lines. The pain is the point.

### Scaling ladder (matches "if we're gaining traction")
| Tier | When | Content |
|---|---|---|
| **1 — Street vox pop** | Now | Cheap, phone-shot pain-point interviews (formats 1–4) |
| **2 — Real testimonials** | Once you have happy users | Format 5 |
| **3 — Creator collabs** | Growing reach | Format 6, finfluencer sit-downs |
| **4 — Branded recurring series** | Established | "FinSight on the Street" as a named show |

- **Organic KPI:** shares + "relatable" comments (leading signal), watch-through, follows.
- **Boost-ready?** ✅ The pain-point episodes make excellent *later* ads — the
  problem is stated in a real Filipino's voice, not ours.

## 7. Compliance & copy guardrails (applies to ALL posts and ads)

The product's "not advice" contract is also the content policy. Every post must pass:

| ✅ Do (awareness) | ❌ Never (advice / claims) |
|---|---|
| "Here's what the models say it's worth." | "This stock is a buy." |
| "This news *could* pressure property developers." | "This will go up / down." |
| "Never get blindsided — see the source yourself." | "Guaranteed returns / beat the market." |
| Hedged: *may, could, historically* | Imperatives: *buy, sell, should* |
| Always cite/link the primary source | Predict a price or a timeframe |

- No performance/return promises (also aligns with Meta & TikTok financial ad
  policy — matters the moment you start paid).
- Keep a visible disclaimer: *"For awareness and education. Not investment advice."*

## 8. Paid amplification (LATER — not now)

> You're organic-only today. This is the playbook for when you turn on spend —
> the fastest path is to **boost the organic posts that already won** (highest
> saves/shares), not to build net-new ad creative.

**Trigger to start paid:** once 2–3 organic posts clear a clear engagement bar
(e.g. ▶ >5% save+share rate, or a Reel/TikTok past ~20k organic views) and the app
is converting the organic traffic it already gets.

**How each channel turns on:**

| Channel | First paid move | Audience | Objective |
|---|---|---|---|
| **TikTok** | **Spark Ads** on top organic (TT-1, TT-3) | Broad PH 18–34 + interest: finance/investing | Video views → traffic |
| **Facebook** | Boost FB-1 (awareness) → retarget with FB-2 (conversion) | PSE/COL/personal-finance interests; **OFW geo** for FB-4 | Reach → conversions |
| **Instagram** | Story ad (IG-3) + whitelisted creator Reels (IG-4) | Retarget FB/IG engagers + lookalike of sign-ups | Conversions |

**Suggested first paid split ▶:** 40% TikTok / 35% Facebook / 25% Instagram —
rebalance to whichever hits target CAC.

**Before any spend ▶:** confirm whether Meta/TikTok require financial-services
**advertiser verification/authorization** for PH; set up server-side conversion
events (Meta CAPI / TikTok Events API) for accurate attribution.

## 10. Measurement (organic phase)

- **North-star:** cost-free right now, so track **activated users** (signed up
  **and** ran one valuation or added one holding) attributed to social.
- **Leading content signal:** **saves + shares** on IG/TikTok — these predict
  which posts to keep making (and, later, which to boost).
- **Per platform:** reach, watch-through/ThruPlay, link/bio taps, follower growth.
- **Attribution:** unique UTM per post/series on the bio link; a promo code per
  creator for referral tracking.
- **Weekly review:** double down on the top 20% of posts by saves+shares; retire
  formats that don't move.
